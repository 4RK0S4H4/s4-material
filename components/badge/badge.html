Components / Badge

Badges show that something is new or how many items need attention, on a navigation icon or next to a label. A small dot means "something new"; a number counts items. Badges use error and on-error.

Small and Large

Small is a 6dp dot (an empty element). Large is 16dp high with a count; cap long counts at "999+".

On an Icon

Wrap the 24dp icon in .s4-badge-host. The dot sits in the icon's top-end corner; a count starts 12dp in from the icon's end. Give the control an accessible name that includes the count.

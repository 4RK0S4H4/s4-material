Components / Button Group

Button groups put related buttons together. A standard group spaces buttons 8dp apart and reacts as one: press and hold a button to see it widen while its neighbours give way. A connected group joins buttons into one control for choosing between options, like a segmented button; the selected option morphs fully round.

Standard

Actions for the current bill. Press and hold.

Connected, Single Select

data-connected data-select="single": exactly one option is selected. Tab to the group, then use the arrow keys and Space. Stretches to fill its width by default.

Showing: month

Connected, Multi Select and Fit Content

data-select="multi" toggles each option. data-fit="content" keeps the group as wide as its labels.

Sizes

The group follows its buttons' data-size; inner corners grow with the size.

Disabled Option

A disabled button is skipped by the arrow keys.

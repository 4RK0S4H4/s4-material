Components / Button

Buttons start actions. Use one filled button for the most important action in an area, tonal for a strong secondary action, and outlined or text for the rest. Labels are Title Case and say exactly what happens. Press and hold to see the M3 Expressive shape morph.

Variants

Filled, tonal, outlined, elevated, text. Each with an icon, and disabled.

Sizes

Extra small (32) to extra large (136). Small (40) is the default. Every size keeps a 48dp touch target.

Shape and Toggle

Round by default; data-shape="square" for the square shape. Toggle buttons (data-toggle) switch between a quiet fill and the full color, and turn square when selected.

Destructive

data-color="error" on the confirm button of a destructive dialog or a danger zone. A per-row delete stays a plain icon button.

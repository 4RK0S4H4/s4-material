Components / Checkbox

Checkboxes let people pick any number of options from a list, or confirm one thing before submitting a form. Use a switch instead when a setting applies immediately. Labels are Title Case; a sentence-length label (an agreement) is sentence case.

States

Unchecked, checked, indeterminate, error and disabled.

Group

A parent checkbox shows indeterminate when only some of its children are checked. Try it.

Dialogs ask for a decision. The button you press becomes the dialog (container transform): its shape and color grow into the dialog at real size, the label fades out and the text fades in. Closing runs it back into the button. Focus starts on the first non-destructive action, so Enter never confirms a delete by accident.

Basic and Destructive

Escape, Cancel, or a click on the scrim closes the dialog.

Components / List

Lists show rows of the same kind: bills, vendors, settings. Each item has an optional leading element (icon, avatar, image or checkbox), a headline with optional overline and supporting text, and optional trailing content (supporting text, an icon, a switch, or an amount). Items are 56, 72 or 88 high for one, two or three lines.

One, Two and Three Lines

The height follows the content: a supporting line or overline makes a two-line item, both make three. data-lines forces a height and clamps supporting text.

Controls and Dividers

A label.s4-list-item makes the whole row toggle its checkbox or switch. data-dividers draws a line between items.

Segmented and Grouped

M3 Expressive segmented list (data-style="segmented"): items 2 apart with small inner corners, each group rounded large at its ends. Subheaders group items and can carry a subtotal.

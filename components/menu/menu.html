Components / Menu

Menus show a short list of choices from a button. They unfold from the edge next to their button (the menu’s own shape opens; items don’t move), sit on glass, and close on Escape, a choice, or a click outside. Keyboard: arrows, Home, End, and type to jump.

Menu Over Content

Open each menu; the colorful tiles show what the glass does.

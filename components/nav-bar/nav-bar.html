Components / Navigation Bar

The navigation bar switches between 3 to 5 top-level destinations on phones and small tablets (under 840 wide; from 840 the navigation rail takes over). The current destination has aria-current="page": its pill indicator expands in and its label turns bold. In an app shell it sticks to the bottom and pads for the home indicator.

Compact

80dp, icon in a 56×32 pill with the label under it. Tap another destination to see the indicator expand. A badge goes in the indicator (.s4-badge-host around the icon).

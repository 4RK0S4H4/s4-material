Components / Snackbar

Snackbars confirm an action in a short line, often with Undo. They slide up from the bottom edge, stay above a nav bar or FAB, pause while you hover or focus them, and a new one replaces the old. They stay solid (inverse surface) so the action color keeps its contrast.

Messages

Each button shows a different snackbar.

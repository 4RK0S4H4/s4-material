Components / Switch

Switches turn a single setting on or off, and the change takes effect immediately, like turning on Autopay. Use a checkbox instead when the choice is only applied after the person presses Save. The label says what is on, in Title Case.

States

Off and on, with and without icons, and disabled. Press and hold to see the handle grow; tab to see the focus ring.

Settings Rows

Put the label first to end-align the switch, as in a settings list.

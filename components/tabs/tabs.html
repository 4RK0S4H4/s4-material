Components / Tabs

Tabs switch between related views of the same content at the same level, such as a list of bills by status. Primary tabs sit under the top app bar and organize the whole screen; secondary tabs sit inside a content area and divide it further. Use the arrow keys, Home and End: the selection follows focus.

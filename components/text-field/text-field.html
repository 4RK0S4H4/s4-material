Components / Text Field

Text fields let people enter and edit text. One look, the Quiet Box: a thin outline, medium corners and the label above the box, always visible. Focus turns the outline into a 2dp ink ring; hover darkens it. Labels are Title Case, helper text is a short sentence. Numbers use tabular figures.

Fields

Prefix and suffix are always shown. Leading and trailing icons, a select and a growing textarea with a counter.

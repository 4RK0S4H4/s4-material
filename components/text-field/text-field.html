Components / Text Field

Text fields let people enter and edit text. Filled fields stand out in forms and dialogs; outlined fields are lighter and suit dense forms. Both use a floating label, which shrinks above the text once the field has focus or a value. Labels are Title Case, helper text is a short sentence. Numbers use tabular figures.

Filled

Default. Prefix and suffix appear once the label floats. Leading and trailing icons, a select and a growing textarea with a counter.

Components / Toolbar

Toolbars hold the actions for the current page or selection. The floating toolbar is a pill that floats over content, often beside a FAB; the docked toolbar spans the bottom edge, in place of a navigation bar on pages that have none. With role="toolbar" the whole toolbar is one tab stop and the arrow keys move between its actions.

Floating

Standard (surface-container) and vibrant (data-color="vibrant", primary-container). A divider can group actions.

Vertical

data-orientation="vertical": a 64dp column, for the side of a wide screen. Up and Down move focus.

With a FAB

.s4-toolbar-dock sets a toolbar beside the FAB. With data-position="bottom" the pair floats centered above the bottom edge (and above the nav bar in an app shell). Scroll the frame.

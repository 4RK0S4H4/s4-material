Components / Top App Bar

The top app bar shows the screen's title, a navigation button and up to three actions. Small is the default for most screens. Medium and large give a top-level screen a bigger headline that collapses into the small bar as you scroll. At rest the bar matches the page (surface); once content scrolls under it, it turns surface-container. Scroll each frame to see it.

Small

64dp, title-large. A leading navigation button, an optional subtitle, and trailing actions. data-align="center" centers a short title.

Medium and Large

Medium (112dp, headline-medium) and large (120dp, display-small); a subtitle adds a line. The headline scrolls away under the 64dp row, then a small title fades in and the bar turns surface-container.

# 0007: Icon Lab answers (2026-11-07)

- **Menu button: Dots.** The semantic name `menu` maps to Solar `menu-dots` (was the boxed `hamburger-menu`). It is the same
  glyph as `more`; the two are told apart by context (rail/top bar toggle vs. overflow menu) and by their aria-labels.
- **Glyphs in controls (add, close, check): not answered.** Unchanged: boxed `add-circle`, `close-circle`, `check-circle`.
  The lab recommended plain glyphs; reopen only if the owner asks.

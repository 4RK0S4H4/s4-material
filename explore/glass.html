Color

Glass in S4 Orion sits only on floating chrome: the app bar once content scrolls under it, the nav bar, toolbars and overlays. Each preview below is a small bills app; scroll inside it to slide the colorful content under the glass, and switch theme and color at the top.

Every material option shows its guaranteed contrast: the worst case for text on that glass when what’s behind it is pure black or pure white. Orion only allows settings that keep text at WCAG AA (4.5:1) over any backdrop. Pick one option per question by tapping its card (not the preview). Answers are saved with your other labs.

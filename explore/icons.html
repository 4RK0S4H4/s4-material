S4 Orion: Icon Lab

Theme

Solar’s Bold Duotone set draws some everyday glyphs inside their own circle or rounded square: add, close, check and menu. Inside a FAB, a button or an icon button that gives a shape inside a shape, and at 24px the close and add circles read as grey dots.

Both options stay in Solar Bold Duotone (arrows stay Linear); only the glyph behind four names changes. Tap the card (above its preview) to pick one; answers are saved with your other labs.

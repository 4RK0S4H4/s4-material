S4 Orion: Liquid Glass Lab 3

Theme

You liked how the WebGL liquid glass looks and want some of it in place of today's blur. WebGL can only bend a canvas, and an app's page isn't one, so this lab tries another way that also runs on iPhone: a glass surface holds a live copy of the content behind it. The middle of the copy is blurred (like today); the rim band holds a magnified copy, which makes content bend at the edge like a thick piece of glass. No backdrop-filter and no WebGL.

Scroll the feed inside each phone: the glass follows it. Try it on your iPhone; this is the part I can't test in Safari. Cost: the page behind is duplicated inside each glass surface, so it suits a few chrome surfaces (nav bar, toolbar, menu), not everything.

Tap a card (above its preview) to pick one; Other plus a note if none is right.

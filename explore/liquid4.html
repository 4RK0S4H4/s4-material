S4 Orion: Liquid Glass Lab 4

Theme

The WebGL glass from the Liquid Glass Lab works because that lab's whole phone is a picture drawn into a canvas, which WebGL can read. A real app's page is DOM, which WebGL can't read directly. This lab closes that gap: the page stays real, live DOM, a snapshot of it (drawn once, redrawn when the theme changes) is the texture, and the same WebGL glass is drawn on top of it where the nav bar and top bar are.

Open this on your iPhone. The thing I can't test is whether Safari draws the snapshot (it renders the page into an SVG image, and WebKit can be fussy about fonts and images). If the glass shows, bends and follows your scrolling, it works. Under each phone it says how long the snapshot took.

Tap a card (above its preview) to pick one; Other plus a note if none is right. The device question near the end is the important one.

S4 Orion: Liquid Glass Lab 5

Theme

You liked the Liquid Glass Lab, but its bend only worked over a canvas or in Chrome. This is a new way that should work on iPhone: each glass bar holds a live, invisible copy of the content behind it, and the lab's refraction (the same lens math) is applied to that copy as an SVG filter. Safari supports that on normal elements. What it can't do is backdrop-filter, which is why the earlier labs failed there.

These are the real Orion top app bar, nav bar and floating toolbar. Text keeps the AA guarantee: the middle of every bar has today's tint and contrast squeeze. Only the outer 12px band, where the glass bends and no text sits, can be clearer (C, D).

Please scroll inside each phone on your iPhone and say whether it bends, follows smoothly, and which look you like. Tap a card's title to pick it. Open one full screen for a closer look.

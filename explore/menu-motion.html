Theme

You remembered wanting the drop-down to feel more like a container transform. The history: in round one (0008) menus grew out of their button's corner; in round two (0010) I made that quieter (revealed from half height, 6px rise), and the quieter one is what ships now. These three are the real menus on the real motion code; tap the button or the dots in each preview to open and close it.

A. Quiet Unfold is today. B. Grow From the Button is the 0008 one: the menu starts as the button's shape and grows. C. Container Transform is the dialogs' motion: the button itself becomes the menu (its label and icon ride into it and fade, color fades linearly, glass turns clear after landing). Try each at least twice, and on your iPhone.

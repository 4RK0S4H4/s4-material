Seed

Tap a feel card on the right to preview it and pick it (picked cards turn blue). The demos on the left are the preview: play with them or press Play All. Every movement uses one of six springs: three spatial (things that move or resize) and three effects (fades and color, never bouncy). Tune stiffness, bounce and speed under the cards; Pick My Tuned Values saves exactly what you set. Answers are collected under Your Answers at the bottom.

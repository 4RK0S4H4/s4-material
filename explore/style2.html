Round two, from your last notes: a button group that is its own thing, sliders that are ours (with some transparency and glass), a better answer than the bottom sheet on a phone, and glass with a thick, refracting edge.

About the glass: real refraction (content bending at the rim) can only be drawn from a canvas backdrop in Safari and iPhone; over normal page content it needs an SVG filter that only Chrome renders. So the glass options here are CSS looks that work everywhere: a very light edge and a rim that is clearer and brighter than the middle, like the edge of a thick pane. The old Liquid Glass Lab shows true refraction for comparison.

Tap the card (above its preview) to pick one; Other plus a note if none is right. Answers are saved with your other labs.

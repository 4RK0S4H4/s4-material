“Origin” is one idea for showing where a surface comes from: it grows the surface out of the button you pressed. S4 Material did it by scaling the whole surface, so text squashes and stretches on the way in, which is where it can feel goofy. This lab puts that next to calmer ways of saying the same thing and next to the other motion concepts M3 uses, one surface type at a time.

Each option card is an answer: tap its title or frame to pick it (it turns blue). The dashed Preview inside is a tiny app and never picks: tap its button, or ▶ Play, to see the transition in and out. You can pick a different concept for each surface type. Everything runs on the springs you chose (M3 Expressive); the blue controls at the top switch to Fluid or Slow ×3 to compare.

S4 Orion: Transparency Lab

Theme

Real edge-bending glass can't run over a normal page on iPhone, so this lab explores what can: plain CSS glass (backdrop-filter, no WebGL, works in Safari) in a few ways, all at roughly today's blur level. They differ in how much of the content shows through and in how the edge is drawn.

Scroll inside each phone: bright cards, a white card and a black card pass under the nav bar and top bar. Check how much shows through, whether the text stays easy to read, and how the edge feels. Any option you pick would still have to pass Orion's text-contrast check before it ships.

Tap a card (above its preview) to pick one; Other plus a note if none is right.

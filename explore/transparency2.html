S4 Orion: Transparency Lab 2

Theme

Direction from the last round: no shiny or glowing edges. Instead, a very thin line that is just slightly more frosted than the glass itself, the way most apps draw a translucent panel edge. Every phone here is plain CSS (Safari-safe) with the same glass fill (about 38% tint, 10px blur); only the edge changes.

Scroll inside each phone and look at both light and dark mode (the line is milky white in dark mode and also needs to show over the white card). Tap a card (above its preview) to pick one; Other plus a note if none is right.

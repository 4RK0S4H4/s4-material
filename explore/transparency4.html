Theme

The rule as I understand it: items on glass keep their own design and color, with the fill made a bit translucent. No outlines and no frost lines, except on items that are only an outline with no fill (the outlined button, an unselected chip). Text fields therefore lose their outline and are just a translucent fill; the switch loses its ring; filled and tonal buttons were never outlined.

The one thing left to choose is how those outline-only items draw their outline: their own grey one (B) or the glass's frost line (C). The Fill buttons in the bar (70, 55, 40%) change how much of each item's color stays; answer the fill question at the end. If this matches what you want, the summary at the bottom is exactly what I will build; say so (or what to change) in the notes.

What I Will Build

Nothing is built until you confirm. On confirmation, in glass.css (so it applies to everything inside any glass surface, nowhere else):

  1. Buttons (filled, tonal, elevated, outlined fill), chips and text fields on glass draw their own color at the chosen strength (default 55%) over the blurred backdrop instead of a solid fill.
  2. Text fields and the switch lose their outline; nothing else gets one.
  3. Outlined buttons and unselected chips keep an outline (own or frost line, as you pick).
  4. Text stays on-surface; the button label colors are unchanged. I will extend the glass check to the translucent filled buttons and raise their strength if any scheme falls under 4.5:1.
  5. Solid wherever glass is solid (high contrast, reduced transparency, no backdrop-filter, data-glass="off"). Record as decision 0015, update docs and the skill.

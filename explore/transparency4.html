Your note: the glass is there to show depth, but your own UI stays the main thing on top of it, so items on glass should not all get frost lines. Here every item keeps its own design and outline, and its fill is just made a bit translucent so the blurred backdrop shows through. Frost lines appear only on a very few items (D and E), where an edge helps.

Items stay inside the glass's guarantee: their text is still on-surface and their fills are light, so the text contrast on glass has to be re-checked for whichever you pick. Tap a card (above its preview) to pick it, tap again to unpick. Try typing in the fields.

Theme

Your note: the colors coming through the glass hit too hard, so make glass more frosted: more blur and calmer color. Today's glass is a 16px blur with color boosted to 180%; that boost is what makes the backdrop's colors so strong. These five cards change only blur and color strength (plus the tint on E); the frost line from decision 0013 and no shadow stay.

Good news for the guarantee: blur and color strength don't affect the text-contrast check (that depends on tint and the contrast squeeze), so frostier is free. Each card shows its text contrast for the monochrome theme (the full check runs 97 color schemes and is stricter). The sliders in the bar add or remove blur and color on every card at once, so you can overshoot and tell me where it stops looking right. Tap a card to pick it, tap again to unpick.

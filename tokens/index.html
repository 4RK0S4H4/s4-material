Seed

Every value in S4 Orion, rendered straight from tokens.css, which is generated from tokens/src/*.tokens.json. Switch theme and contrast to see each mode; the seed buttons preview the run-time color generator (any other seed builds the same roles). Values shown are read live from the page.

Color

The default monochrome scheme from Material Color Utilities. Each swatch is a role with its matching “on” role, the contrast ratio measured live, and the ratio it needs in brackets (4.5 for text, 3 for outlines). The build fails if any pair in the default scheme drops below WCAG AA.

Typography

Inter on the M3 type scale. Emphasized weights (M3 Expressive) appear in the right column of each row.

Shape

The stock M3 Expressive corner scale, then which step each component uses.

Spacing and Size

A 4dp grid. Component sizes are M3's; every interactive target is at least 48dp.

Elevation

M3 shows depth with tonal surfaces first; shadows are for FABs, menus and elevated components.

Motion

M3 Expressive springs, turned into CSS linear() easings by the build. Spatial springs move things and may overshoot; effects springs fade and recolor and never do; calm springs land exactly on their target (container transforms collapsing back into their source). Reduced motion makes spatial springs instant.

State

M3 state layers in the content color, the focus ring (3dp secondary, 2dp outside) and disabled opacities.

Glass

Very clear glass (decision 0004): 60% of surface-container over a 16px blur with saturation, plus a gentle compression of what shows through so text on it meets WCAG AA over any backdrop, for every seed. A hairline marks the edge that faces content. Scroll the list under the bar. High contrast, reduced transparency and browsers without blur turn it solid.
